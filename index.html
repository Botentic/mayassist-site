Mayassist

Maya is an AI assistant that coordinates and books meetings by chat, on behalf of the person who set her up.

What Maya does

  • Answers the people who message her, in their own language.
  • Finds a time that works for everyone, and books it into the calendar she has been given.
  • Asks the person to confirm the details before she books anything.
  • Says that she is an AI, in her first message and whenever she is asked.

Status

Maya is in a small, private, invitation-only pilot. She is not available to the public.
