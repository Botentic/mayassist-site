Maya and your privacy

Last updated: 13 November 2026 · בעברית

Hi, I'm Maya. I'm an AI assistant, not a person. I answer WhatsApp messages and arrange meetings for the person who set me up. I say so in my first message to you, and whenever you ask.

Maya runs in a pilot of Mayassist, a project run by its founder, Ori Atary, in Israel, who is responsible for this data.

What we keep

On the project's server:

  • your WhatsApp number and the name on your WhatsApp profile;
  • the messages you send Maya, and her replies;
  • a short summary of your conversations, so Maya remembers the context next time;
  • a record of what Maya did, such as a meeting she booked.

Why

To answer you, arrange your meetings and keep the service working. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

Who else handles it

  • OpenAI (United States): its AI models read your messages so Maya can understand them and reply.
  • Google Calendar: when a meeting is booked, your name and the meeting details go into the calendar of the person Maya works for.
  • WhatsApp carries the messages, under its own terms.
  • Ori Atary, who runs the pilot, is the only person who can read conversations, and does so only to fix problems. Maya passes on to the person she works for what they need, such as your booking or a question she cannot answer.

If you connect your Google Calendar

Maya reads its events to find free times, and adds the meetings she books. Event details may be read by OpenAI's AI models so Maya can plan. We use Google data only to run Maya for you, never for advertising or AI training. Mayassist's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can remove Maya's access in your Google Account at any time.

How long

  • Messages: 180 days, then deleted.
  • Summaries and the record of what Maya did: until you ask us to delete them.
  • When the pilot ends, all of the above is deleted within 14 days.
  • A technical log keeps ids and times, never message text, for 90 days.
  • Bookings stay in the calendar of the person Maya works for, as their own record.

Seeing, correcting or deleting your data

You can ask to see or correct what we keep about you. You don't have to give Maya any personal information.

To delete your data, write to team@mayassist.ai and say which WhatsApp number you used. Within 7 days, we delete your messages, summaries and records, and tell you when it is done. Your number stays on the list of people Maya answers, so she can still reply if you write again. If you also want it removed from that list, say so and we remove it too.
